## Service 1 — Nature Tours: Waterfalls, Forests & Summits

Set off to discover Togo's natural wonders: the Kpime waterfalls cascading through lush greenery, the dense Missahohe forest alive with rare birdsong, and Mount Agou — Togo's highest peak — offering a breathtaking panoramic view. Every excursion is an invitation to reconnect with the raw, generous nature of West Africa.

- Guided hikes to Kpime and Akloa waterfalls
- Mount Agou summit trek with certified guide
- Exploration of the classified Missahohe forest

*Why Afriroad? Our local naturalist guides know every trail and species — your safety and sense of wonder are our absolute priority.*

## Service 2 — Cultural Circuits: Villages, Traditions & Heritage

Dive into the soul of Togo through its traditional villages, ancestral ceremonies, and vibrant markets. From the UNESCO-listed Batammariba earth fortresses to the voodoo rituals of the coast, every stop on our cultural circuits is a living history lesson and an unforgettable human encounter.

- Visit to the Tamberma castles (Koutammakou, UNESCO World Heritage)
- Immersion in traditional ceremonies and local markets
- Authentic meetings with local communities

*Why Afriroad? We work directly with village chiefs and communities to offer you a respectful and deeply genuine experience.*
