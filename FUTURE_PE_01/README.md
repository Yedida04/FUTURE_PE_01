## Prompt Logic Summary

**Prompt 1 — Homepage**
Providing visual identity, brand colors and 3 audience profiles forces
the AI to produce brand-specific content instead of generic text.
Requesting 5 numbered output blocks ensures structured, usable results.

**Prompt 2 — Services**
Naming real Togolese locations (Kpime, Missahohe, Mount Agou,
Koutammakou) grounds the content in local authenticity and credibility.
A "Why Afriroad" sentence per service acts as a micro-differentiator.

**Prompt 3 — CTA**
Segmenting CTAs by visitor psychology (ready to book / hesitant /
local resident) improves conversion potential versus a single generic CTA.
Each CTA targets a different trigger: urgency, social proof, local belonging.
